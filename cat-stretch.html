Jump to cursor: how far away the cursor may be (in cat lengths) for the cat to jump to it. In Plash the cursor reaches the cat only in Browsing Mode.

Timing

Run speed: faster runs take quicker, longer strides with longer flights. Jump crouch: how long the cat crouches and wiggles its rump before it jumps. Jump height: how high it flies (1× = normal; farther jumps go higher, never past the top of the screen; higher jumps stay longer in the air and tilt steeper, up to 70°).

Emotion

Round

Drag a dot: it snaps to the 20 × 20 grid. Pupil size 0 hides the pupil (the winking eye). Click a dot to set its roundness (0 sharp, 1 round). Blue dots are corners, pink dots sit on the centre lines; where dots are stacked, drag up for the top one, down for the bottom one. The cat shows this emotion while the Eyes tab is open; Sleeping is the closed eye that blinks, naps, the stretch and licking turn every emotion into.

Leg points

Every leg has points 1 shoulder / hip, 2 first fold, 3 second fold, 4 paw. With the skeleton shown, drag a point or its number: only that point of that leg moves, snapped to the grid (hold ⌥ to place it freely). Purple: front legs, orange: hind legs; filled numbers are the near side, outlined the far side.

Spine

The shoulder circles (front) and the hip circles (back) set the body's form; the back and belly lines join them. Spine curve: 0 straight, + arches the back up, - sags it toward the ground.

Shoulders & front legs

Facing you, fwd/back does nothing: it is toward you.

The shoulder circles (purple dashed) are the front of the body: 1 size is its depth there, the dashed line between them the distance, and each front leg starts from its circle. These sliders move both front legs: 2 on its own, 3–4 together (the leg follows). To move one leg, drag its points.

Hips & hind legs

The hip circles are the cat's behind; the body lines leave from their edges. The dashed line between them is the distance; the small pink circle is where the tail joins. These sliders move both hind legs: 2 on its own, 3–4 together (the leg follows). Sitting, 2 is the knee: it folds forward and can go up past the hip.

All legs & paws

A paw never goes further than the leg reaches, and a paw moved down rests on the floor.

Tail

Each position keeps its own shape. Moves between positions blend from one shape to the next.
